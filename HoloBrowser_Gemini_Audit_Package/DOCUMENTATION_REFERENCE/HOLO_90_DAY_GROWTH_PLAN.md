# Holo Browser 1.0 — 90-Day Growth Engine Plan

**Author**: Growth Engineer & Founder  
**Date**: July 30, 2026

## 1. 90-Day Growth Execution Timeline

### Phase 1: Launch Week (Days 1–7) — First 100 Active Users
- **Launch Product Hunt & Show HN**: Publish launch announcements on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit (`r/macapps`, `r/privacy`), and X.
- **Goal**: Reach 100+ daily active beta users and collect early feedback logs (`Preferences > Diagnostics`).

### Phase 2: Community Iteration (Days 8–30) — First 500 Active Users
- **Sparkle Micro-Updates**: Deliver bi-weekly performance patches based on beta feedback.
- **Word-of-Mouth Campaigns**: Encourage Mac developer referrals and publish benchmark comparisons vs Arc/Chrome.

### Phase 3: Scaling & Platform Expansion (Days 31–90) — Version 1.1 Launch
- **Launch Version 1.1**: Roll out Smart Tab Auto-Grouper, Privacy Dashboard, and Research Workspace enhancements.
- **Mac App Store & Setapp**: Submit app bundle for Mac App Store review and Setapp network inclusion.
