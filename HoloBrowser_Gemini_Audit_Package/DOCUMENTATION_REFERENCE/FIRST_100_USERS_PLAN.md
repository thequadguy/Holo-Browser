# Holo Browser 1.0 — First 100 Users Acquisition & Retention Plan

**Author**: Growth Engineer & Founder  
**Date**: July 30, 2026  

---

## 1. User Milestone Growth Strategy

### Day 1 Target: First 25 Active Users
- **Primary Channels**: Show HN post on Hacker News + Product Hunt launch.
- **Goal**: Initial feedback validation and Gatekeeper installation verification.

### Day 7 Target: First 100 Active Users
- **Primary Channels**: Reddit (`r/macapps`, `r/privacy`, `r/LocalLLaMA`) + X social posts.
- **Goal**: Onboarding completion > 85%, diagnostic log feedback collection, and Day 7 retention > 40%.

### Day 30 Target: First 500 Active Users
- **Primary Channels**: Organic word-of-mouth recommendations, blog reviews, and version 1.0.1 maintenance release via Sparkle.
- **Goal**: Established community of daily-driver Mac power users.
