## 2. First 100 Beta Users Target Strategy

- **Target Persona**: Mac developers, privacy advocates, and AI power users who currently alternate between Arc and Safari.
- **Onboarding Invitation**: Provide direct download links (`HoloBrowser.dmg`) with a welcome message inviting them to join the `beta@holobrowser.com` private feedback channel.
- **Retention Strategy**: Bi-weekly beta updates with clear release notes, direct bug fix turnarounds within 24 hours, and a public roadmap board.
