# Holo Browser 1.0 — Support & Troubleshooting Guide

### 1. Gatekeeper Prompts on First Launch
Holo Browser is signed with a Developer ID Application certificate and notarized by Apple. If macOS presents a security dialog, click **Open** or navigate to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

### 2. Exporting Privacy-Sanitized Diagnostics
Open **Preferences > Diagnostics** or click **Export Diagnostics** in `BetaStatusView.swift` to copy a sanitized text report to clipboard.
