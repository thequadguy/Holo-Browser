# HOLO BROWSER — BETA USER INTERVIEW & FEEDBACK QUESTIONS

## 🎙️ Weekly Tester Interview Template

1. **First Impressions**: How did the first 5 minutes feel compared to your primary browser (Safari/Chrome/Arc)?
2. **Speed & Performance**: Did Holo Browser feel fast and responsive? Did you experience any page lag or high fan noise on Intel/Apple Silicon Macs?
3. **HoloMind AI Assistance**: How often did you use the `h ` prefix in the address bar or `⌘K` for AI actions? Were the page summaries helpful?
4. **Command Palette (`⌘K`)**: Did the keyboard-first navigation meet your expectations? Was jumping between open tabs intuitive?
5. **Privacy & Profile Spaces**: How clear was the distinction between Personal, Work, and Private Space modes?
6. **Blockers & Bugs**: Did you encounter any webpage rendering issues, broken logins, or missing downloads?
