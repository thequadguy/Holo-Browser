# Holo Browser: User Experience (UX) Polish Audit & Design Audit Report

---

## 1. Interface & Design System Consistency
* **Liquid Glass Vibrancy**: Visual effect materials (`.sidebar`, `.hudWindow`, `.header`) unified with standard corner radii (`6pt`, `8pt`, `12pt`) and spring animations (`springNormal`).
* **Toolbar & Spacing**: Navigation controls, omnibox address bar, and extension buttons padded uniformly (`10pt` horizontal, `6pt` vertical).

---

## 2. Component Audits
* **Empty States**: Created clean, vector-icon-based empty states across History, Bookmarks, Reading List, Downloads, AI Memories, and Workspaces.
* **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Unified `Cmd+L` (Address Bar Focus), `Cmd+K` (Command Palette), `Cmd+Shift+A` (AI Sidebar), `Cmd+T` (New Tab), `Cmd+W` (Close Tab), `Cmd+Shift+T` (Restore Tab), `Cmd+Option+I` (Web Inspector), and `Cmd+1..9` (Switch Tabs).
* **Onboarding & Settings**: Integrated `WelcomeView` first-launch onboarding modal and unified 8-tab `SettingsView`.
