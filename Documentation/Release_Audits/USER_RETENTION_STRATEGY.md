# Holo Browser — User Retention System & Strategy

**Author**: Head of Product & User Retention Lead  
**Date**: July 30, 2026  

---

## 1. User Retention Journey

### Day 1: Immediate Value (First Impression)
- **Sub-0.5s Launch & Zero RAM Bloat**: Users immediately feel the speed and native Mac responsiveness compared to Chrome/Arc.
- **Cmd + K Command Palette**: Users adopt Spotlight tab switching on day 1.

### Week 1: Habit Formation
- **Profile Switching**: Users organize Work vs Personal tabs under distinct profiles.
- **AI Page Summaries**: Users integrate 1-click page summarization into their daily reading routine.

### Month 1: Advocacy & Recommendation
- **Zero Privacy Worries**: Users trust Holo Browser's local Keychain credential security and regex context scrubbing.
- **Referral & Recommendation**: Users actively recommend Holo Browser to fellow Mac developers and privacy advocates.

## 2. Retention Metrics & Targets

- **Day 1 Retention Target**: > 65%
- **Day 7 Retention Target**: > 45%
- **Day 30 Retention Target**: > 30%
