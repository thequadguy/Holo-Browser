# Holo Browser — Sustainable Privacy-First Business Model Strategy

**Author**: Founder & Growth Strategist  
**Date**: July 30, 2026  

---

## 1. Monetization Principles

- **Zero User Data Sales**: Holo Browser will NEVER monetize by selling user browsing history, search terms, or telemetry data.
- **Zero Telemetry Ads**: No tracking pixels or ad networks embedded in the application.

---

## 2. Revenue Tier Architecture

1. **Holo Free (Public Beta & Core App)**:
   - Full native browser, profile data isolation, Spotlight Command Palette, local Ollama AI integration, and privacy pipeline.
2. **Holo Pro Subscription ($8/month or $72/year)**:
   - Unlimited cloud AI provider access (OpenAI/Anthropic keys hosted or API usage credits).
   - Advanced multi-tab research synthesis and PDF document analysis.
3. **Holo Enterprise Edition ($15/user/month)**:
   - Enterprise security policy enforcement, centralized Keychain credential policy, and custom local AI deployment endpoints.
4. **Setapp Subscription Revenue**:
   - Inclusion in the Setapp Mac subscriber network for recurring revenue sharing.
